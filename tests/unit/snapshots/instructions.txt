## screen/windows/chrome
# Share your screen (Windows)
- Press the green Select screen to share button.
- In the window that opens, choose the Entire screen tab.
- Click the picture of your screen.
- Tick Also share system audio if you want your technician to hear sound from your computer.
- Click Share.
audio: Sound from your computer is shared if you ticked Also share system audio.

## screen/macos/chrome
# Share your screen (Mac)
- Press the green Select screen to share button.
- Choose the Entire screen tab, click the picture of your screen, then click Share.
- If you see a message about Screen Recording, open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Screen and System Audio Recording (called Screen Recording on older versions), and turn your browser on.
- Your Mac will ask you to quit and reopen the browser. Do that, then open this page again. You won&#39;t need a new code.
audio: On a Mac, sound can usually only be shared from a single Chrome tab. If your technician needs to hear something, ask them how best to do it.
note: The first time you share your screen on a Mac you have to give your browser permission. This is a normal Mac security step.

## screen/macos/safari
# Share your screen (Mac, Safari)
- Press the green Select screen to share button.
- Safari asks whether to share a window or your whole screen. Choose Share Entire Screen (or the window your technician asked for), then click Allow.
- If you see a message about Screen Recording, open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Screen and System Audio Recording, and turn Safari on. Quit and reopen Safari when asked.
audio: Safari can&#39;t share sound. Your technician will see your screen but won&#39;t hear it.

## screen/linux/chrome
# Share your screen (Linux)
- Press the green Select screen to share button.
- Choose Entire screen, click the picture of your screen, then click Share.
- On some Linux desktops (Wayland) a second window from your system appears. Pick the screen there and click Share.
audio: Sound can only be shared from a single Chrome tab on Linux.

## screen/android/chrome
# Share your screen (Android)
- Press the green Select screen to share button.
- When your phone asks, choose to share your entire screen, then tap Start now.
- To stop, come back here and press Stop sharing, or use the notification at the top of your screen.

## camera/ios/safari
# Share your camera (iPhone or iPad)
- When asked, tap Allow so this page can use your camera and microphone.
- Point the camera at what your technician needs to see. Use the camera button to switch between front and back cameras.
- If you tapped Don&#39;t Allow by mistake, open Settings, then Safari, then Camera, choose Ask or Allow, and reload this page.
audio: Your technician can hear you through your microphone.
